# Everence Wealth — 25 Recruiting Cluster Seeds (Clusters 51-75)

**Purpose**: Extends the original 50 cluster seeds to 75 total. Adds 5 new themes (11-15) covering insurance industry recruiting, career education, and dual-purpose client/recruit content.

**Compliance posture**: NO money claims. NO income promises. NO "earn $X" framing. NO "best-paying specialty" language. All content is educational and informational. Professional tone matching the existing Everence Wealth voice.

**Audience**: Career changers, aspiring insurance professionals, pre-licensees, new agents, and industry-curious professionals. Some clusters serve dual purposes — clients researching what kind of advisor they're working with also benefit from understanding the industry structure.

**Money page targets**: All BOFU links point to `/contracting/intake` (the existing Everence contracting funnel) or `/careers` if that hub exists. Lovable will verify and remap as needed.

**Last Updated**: 2026-04-27

## Compliance scrub — what's NOT in these clusters

To stay compliant with insurance industry rules and avoid misleading recruiting content, none of these 25 clusters mention:

- Specific income figures, ranges, or earning potential
- "Make $X" or "earn up to $X" framing
- Comparisons of insurance income vs other professions
- "Top-earning specialties" or "best-paying" language
- Promises of financial success or wealth-building through the career
- Guarantees about commission structure
- Sign-on bonuses, override schedules, or compensation specifics

What IS covered: career structure, licensing path, skills development, mentorship, professional growth, work flexibility (autonomy, not income), industry roles, helping families, mission-driven work.

If any individual article generated from these clusters drifts into income claims during the LLM build, the existing `enforce_fiduciary_term_block` compliance trigger plus a manual review pass will catch it before publication.
